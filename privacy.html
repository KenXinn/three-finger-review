Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 • Entity: Three Finger Review

1. Overview

Three Finger Review operates a personal desktop content publishing tool solely to manage and publish original product showcase content (images, titles, and descriptions) to our own authorized accounts.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • No Third-Party User Data: This application is strictly for single-owner personal use and does not accept public registrations or collect personal information from external users.
  • API Authorization Tokens: OAuth access tokens granted by the account owner are stored locally on the owner's private computer and are used exclusively to read the owner's board list and publish Pins on the owner's behalf.
  • No Data Sharing or Selling: We never sell, rent, or transfer any account data or tokens to third parties.

3. Data Retention & Revocation

Locally stored tokens remain on the owner's device only until deleted by the owner or revoked directly through the connected platform's security settings.

4. Affiliate Disclosure

Published product showcases may contain affiliate links to online retailers. We may earn a small commission at no extra cost to buyers.
